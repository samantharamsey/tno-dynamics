## Research and teaching {#research-and-teaching}

<article class="cv-entry">
  <header class="cv-entry__header">
    <div>
      <h3>Graduate Research Assistant</h3>
      <p class="cv-organization">University of Tennessee · Dr. James Evans Lyne</p>
    </div>
    <p class="cv-date">August 2025–present · Knoxville, Tennessee</p>
  </header>
  <ul>
    <li>Developing a phase-space framework to classify resonant and chaotic behavior of trans-Neptunian objects in multibody dynamical models.</li>
    <li>Using Poincaré maps, resonance analysis, and chaos indicators to characterize dynamical structures and long-term orbital evolution.</li>
    <li>Creating Python propagation and analysis workflows to study resonance structure, resonance sticking, chaotic diffusion, and long-term evolution in the trans-Neptunian region.</li>
    <li>Built a generalized differential-corrections targeting framework for periodic-orbit and resonant structures, with configurable free variables, terminal constraints, event-based propagation, and geometry, period, and Jacobi-constant targeting.</li>
    <li>Implemented pseudo-arclength continuation to trace periodic and resonant solution families through turning points.</li>
    <li><strong>Teaching:</strong> System Dynamics and Spaceflight Mechanics.</li>
  </ul>
</article>
