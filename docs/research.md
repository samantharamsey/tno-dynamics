## Trans-Neptunian object dynamics

Trans-Neptunian objects preserve information about the formation and evolution of the outer Solar System. My research uses dynamical-systems tools to investigate the structures that organize their motion, including resonances, invariant structures, and transport between regions of phase space.

## Circular restricted three-body problem

The circular restricted three-body problem provides a controlled model for studying motion under the influence of two massive bodies. In the Sun–Neptune system, it offers a useful framework for examining allowed regions of motion, equilibrium points, periodic orbits, and the pathways available to small bodies.

## Computational approach

The research combines analytical reasoning with numerical computation and visualization. Interactive tools complement conventional figures by allowing a reader to change parameters and directly observe how the geometry of the system responds.
