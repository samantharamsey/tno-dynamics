  # About
  Researching motion in the outer Solar System
---

I am a PhD researcher studying the dynamics of trans-Neptunian objects. My work uses celestial mechanics, numerical computation, and interactive visualization to understand the structures that govern orbital motion in the outer Solar System.

This website is a living record of that research. It brings together project explanations, interactive tools, publications, presentations, and supporting documentation.
